Why accounting firms are rethinking how they build teams

Protect margin as the firm grows

Adding domestic headcount for every increase in client volume can make growth more expensive than it needs to be.

Keep senior people focused on higher-value work

Partners, managers, and experienced accountants create more value when recurring production work is not consuming their week.

Build capacity before the next peak

A dedicated global team can absorb recurring work year-round, creating more room before busy season or rapid client growth arrives.

Reduce the risk around global hiring

Accounting work involves sensitive financial information. Employment structure, equipment, security, and accountability matter as much as recruiting.
