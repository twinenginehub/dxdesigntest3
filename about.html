The Standard We Wanted Didn't Exist. So We Built It.

DOXA was founded on a simple belief: the principles used to build a strong team should not stop applying because the person works in another country.

Explore Ethical Outsourcing

The standard shouldn't change because the border does.

Global professionals deserve proper employment, benefits, good equipment, development, support, and the opportunity to build a career. Clients need committed people, lower costs, security, transparency, and accountability after the hire. DOXA was built on the belief that those interests should reinforce each other.

DOXA directly employs its professionals. We call them V.I.P.s, because the person behind the role matters. We provide comprehensive benefits. We equip people for secure remote work. We invest in learning and development. And we remain involved after the hire. Those choices influence stability, retention, capability, security, and the experience clients have with their teams.

People-first isn't separate from performance.

Our Purpose

Transform global communities by creating meaningful work.

Our founding belief stayed the same. The work didn't.

DOXA began by challenging how global professionals were employed and supported. Now AI is changing the skills people need, the work they perform, and what businesses expect from their teams. People-first has never meant standing still. DOXA V.I.P.s are in the process of becoming independently AI-certified, building practical capabilities they can apply as the work changes.

The goal is not simply better talent on day one. It is talent that can keep getting better.
