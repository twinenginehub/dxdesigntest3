Straight Answers

Can we use DOXA for just one part of the model?
DOXA is designed as an integrated managed talent model. Recruiting, employment and compliance, security and IT support, and ongoing development work together around the professionals we place and support.
Can we start with one person?
Yes. The threshold is whether the work is defined well enough to hand off, not headcount.
Who manages the person day to day?
You do. You direct the work and set priorities. DOXA manages the employment and operating infrastructure around the professional.
How is DOXA different from an EOR?
An EOR primarily provides the legal employment layer. DOXA also recruits, equips, supports, and develops the professional as part of one managed model.
How is DOXA different from a BPO?
A BPO typically takes responsibility for delivering an outsourced process. DOXA provides dedicated professionals who work inside your team while you retain direction of the work.
