30-day onboarding one-sheet

A one-page plan for the first 30 days of a new dedicated professional: what to prepare, what to do each week, and how to know it is working.

1Before day one

Manager and buddy

  • Accounts and tool access created
  • Equipment shipped and tested
  • Buddy named and introduced
  • First-week schedule sent
  • Role and goals written down

2Week one

Learn the basics

  • Team introductions and norms
  • Walkthrough of tools and process
  • One small first task, done together
  • Questions list started
  • Friday check-in held

3Weeks two and three

Take ownership

  • Shadow a full workflow, then own one piece
  • Documents or checklists reviewed
  • Feedback given within 48 hours
  • Weekly check-in held, twice
  • Blockers raised and cleared

4Week four

Review and plan

  • Day 30 review completed
  • What is working and what is not
  • Goals set for days 31 to 90
  • Support and training needs noted
  • Next review date booked

Weekly check-in: four questions

  1. What went well this week?
  2. Where did you get stuck?
  3. What do you need from me?
  4. What is the priority for next week?
